About Us

A growing provider of online voting solutions.

Who we are

BallotOnline started with a simple idea: taking part in a vote should be as easy as sending a message. Today we serve clients across three continents.

We currently operate our own regional data centers, each with its own servers, networking and storage. As our client base grows, we are evaluating public cloud platforms to make the service more scalable and resilient.
