Services

Everything an organization needs to run an online vote.

What we offer

Our platform supports clients in North America, Europe and Asia with these core features.

1

Candidate profiles

Give voters a short, consistent profile for every candidate on the ballot.

2

Polling

Run quick polls and surveys alongside formal elections.

3

Mobile-friendly ballots

Ballots adapt to any screen, so no one needs a desktop to vote.

4

Write-in candidates

Let voters add a name that is not on the printed ballot.

5

Email notifications

Remind voters when polls open and confirm that their ballot was received.

6

Live results

Publish turnout and final results as soon as voting ends.
